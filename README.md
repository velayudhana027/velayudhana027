## I'm Velayudhan Aravindakshan 👋

Robotics enthusiast with a passion for humanoid robots: how they see, move, and work alongside people.

🎓 M.Sc. Autonomy Technologies, FAU Erlangen-Nürnberg · previously System Engineer at TCS for General Motors (ECU validation, vHIL automation)

-  Manipulation on the **Unitree G1** humanoid
-  Human-robot interaction with **NAO** and the **Franka Emika Panda** arm
-  Robot vision: point feature extraction research and matrix-barcode-based **autonomous UAV landing**
- Right now working with tactile sensing for making artificial skin
